# NEU Student Knowledge Base

This document contains verified student procedures provided by the project owner. The AI should use these answers as the primary reference for common NEU student questions. It must not invent missing procedures. Procedures that depend on current school information, such as the academic calendar, should use the latest approved source.

## 1. Class Schedule
**Question:** Where can I check my class schedule?
**Answer:** Students can check their class schedule through Automate. Automate can only be accessed/opened while at school.

## 2. Student ID
**Question:** How can I get my student ID?
**Answer:** Fill out the Google Form sent to your school email for the student ID request. After submitting the form, wait approximately 1–3 weeks. Once the ID is ready, claim it at the CICS Office.

## 3. Enrollment / Subjects
**Question:** How can I enroll in my subjects for the next semester?
**Answer:** Before enrolling, make sure you have no pending grades and no outstanding balance. Enrollment is done at school, where teachers/guides are available to assist students with the enrollment process.

## 4. Grades
**Question:** Where can I check my grades?
**Answer:** Your professor may send your grades through email. You can also check your grades through Automate.

## 5. Enrollment Status
**Question:** How do I know if I am officially enrolled?
**Answer:** Check your enrollment information through Automate. You can also use your Certificate of Matriculation (COM) as proof of enrollment.

## 6. Tuition / School-Fee Balance
**Question:** Where can I check how much I still need to pay?
**Answer:** Check the payment/receipt information on your Certificate of Matriculation (COM). You can also check your current status through the Clearance tab in Automate.

## 7. Paying Tuition / School Fees
**Question:** How can I pay my tuition or school fees?
**Answer:** Go to the Cashier on the 2nd floor of the Main Building. Present your student ID to the cashier and make the payment. Keep the receipt after completing the transaction.

## 8. Good Moral Certificate
**Question:** How can I request a Good Moral Certificate?
**Answer:** Go to the 2nd floor of the Main Building and proceed to the VP's Office to request the Good Moral Certificate.

## 9. Certificate of Enrollment and Other Official Documents
**Question:** How can I request a Certificate of Enrollment or other official school documents?
**Answer:** Go to the 2nd floor of the Main Building. For a Certificate of Enrollment and other official documents, proceed to the office located beside the VP's Office and submit your request there.

## 10. Lost Student ID
**Question:** What should I do if I lose my student ID?
**Answer:** First, secure an Affidavit of Loss. Present the Affidavit of Loss to the Office of Student Development (OSD). After that, proceed to the Campus Services Department (CSD) to request a replacement student ID.

## 11. School Account / Automate Login
**Question:** I cannot log in to my school account or Automate. What should I do?
**Answer:** Contact your faculty for assistance with the account or Automate login concern.

## 12. School Announcements
**Question:** Where can I check the latest school announcements and important updates?
**Answer:** Check the official New Era University Facebook page for the latest school announcements and updates.

## 13. Academic Calendar
**Question:** Where can I check important dates such as enrollment, exams, holidays, and other academic schedules?
**Answer:** The latest school academic calendar should be used as the reference. The project owner will provide the current calendar for analysis and knowledge-base updates. The AI should not invent academic dates or rely on an outdated calendar.

---

## AI Knowledge-Use Rules
1. Prefer these verified procedures when answering common student questions.
2. Do not invent office locations, requirements, fees, schedules, processing times, or procedures that are not documented here.
3. If a procedure may have changed, tell the student to verify the latest announcement or official school source.
4. For the academic calendar, use the latest approved calendar provided to the system.
5. Keep answers concise and actionable: tell the student where to go, what to prepare, and what to do next when that information is available.
6. Preserve the distinction between Automate, faculty assistance, school offices, and official announcements.
